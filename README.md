# 🌆 CitySense AI — Smart City Explorer

**Explore smarter. Navigate confidently. Experience your city better.**

CitySense AI is an AI-powered smart city exploration platform built for **PromptWars**. It brings city discovery, weather information, cultural attractions, and safety resources together in one interactive experience.

Our goal is to transform scattered urban information into useful insights that help people explore cities more confidently and make informed decisions.

## 🚀 Problem Statement

### City Life: Exploring, Experiencing & Navigating the Chaos We Call Home

Cities offer exciting opportunities to discover local food, historical landmarks, and new experiences. However, navigating unfamiliar places also brings challenges such as unpredictable weather, traffic congestion, affordability, and safety concerns.

CitySense AI aims to simplify city exploration by combining geographic data, weather information, and responsible safety guidance in one platform.

## ✨ Key Features

* 🗺️ **Interactive City Exploration** — Explore cities and discover nearby points of interest.
* 🍽️ **Food & Hospitality** — Find restaurants, cafes, hotels, and other useful locations where data is available.
* 🏛️ **History & Culture** — Discover historical landmarks, heritage sites, and cultural attractions.
* 🌦️ **Weather Intelligence** — View current weather conditions and forecasts from available weather services.
* 🛡️ **Safety Information** — Access relevant official emergency resources and distinguish verified information from unverified reports.
* ⚖️ **City Comparison** — Compare available city information using transparent criteria.
* 📝 **Citizen Reports** — Support community observations with clear submission and verification statuses.
* 🤖 **AI-Powered Assistance** — Help users discover places and plan city experiences.

*Feature availability depends on the current implementation and connected data sources.*

## 🔐 Safety, Privacy & Data Integrity

CitySense AI is designed around responsible presentation of information.

* Citizen submissions should be labeled as unverified until appropriately reviewed.
* Missing incident reports must not be interpreted as proof that an area is safe.
* Safety information should identify its source and date whenever available.
* City comparisons should use transparent criteria rather than unsupported rankings.
* Private credentials must not be exposed in client-side code.
* Users should consult official authorities for urgent safety information.

## 🧑‍💻 Getting Started

### Run the project locally

For a standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript version:

1. Clone or download this repository.

2. Open the project folder in VS Code or Google Antigravity.

3. Open a terminal in the folder.

4. Run:

   ```bash
   py -m http.server 8000
   ```

5. Visit http://localhost:8000.

If the project uses React, Vite, or another framework, follow the instructions in its `package.json` or project setup documentation instead.
